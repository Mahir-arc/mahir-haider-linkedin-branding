Meet your physician

Experience guided by a thoughtful approach.

“The right care begins with listening, understanding and a clear plan.”

Dr. Babak Larian is a board-certified otolaryngologist and head & neck surgeon, and the founder of the Center for Advanced Head & Neck Surgery in Beverly Hills.

His professional background includes medical education at the University of California, Irvine, residency training at UCLA Medical Center and clinical faculty involvement with the David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA.
